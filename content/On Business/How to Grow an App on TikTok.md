title: How To Grow an App on TikTok Without Getting Lucky.
---

All the below tips are very useful, but it’s the 25%… 75% of the path to success is literally just posting 3x a day everyday, learning from each post and getting better over time. TikTok is a free supermarket - the more you go, the more you’re gonna get, but you still have to show up to get stuff. You still have to post a lot consistently to get growth on TikTok regardless of account size.

If you’re just starting out, do this and only this the first week or two:

1. Search for terms and tags of your niche community and scroll for at least 2 hours, through all the content that exists in your space. You should scroll so much, that you are legit sick of watching content about your space at the end. This is training you to be completely in tune with all the nuances of the content that works well in your space - Down to the inside jokes, the lighting, the angles, the popular comments etc etc. You have to do this research, or your content will be slightly off and you’ll have more misses than you need to.
   
2. Just post. In the beginning, you don’t need a content plan (we still don’t have one), any gear, any list of ideas etc. Just post the best idea you have at the time and get over that first post. Everyone massively overcomplicates it in the beginning - All you need to do is scroll the feed to feel what resonates with your community, and then post something based on what you learnt.
   
3. Try to post at least once, ideally twice a day every day. You can work up to 3-4 posts per day in due course, but in the beginning, being consistent with 1-2 is great.
   
4. Any piece of engagement you get, respond to it. Reply to all of your comments, DMs etc. You’re not just engaging the early community, you’re also showing TikTok that you’re present and that they don’t need to put you in a view jail.

So yeah, in the beginning just learn, post, repeat.

Assuming you’re already hitting this, you’ve formed the habit and it’s been a week or two, here is some more tactical stuff to eek out signups/conversions to your product from TikTok:

### **Be Direct, But Not Too "Ad-y"**

- Actually mention your product. Don’t be too subtle. If you’ve got an easy to say URL or app name, actually say it and talk about it. Here is an example at the end of this video: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7447885610737487136](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7447885610737487136) (this post gave us tens of thousands of signups)
  
- Avoid old-school, cringeworthy ads styles though. If your video sounds boomer, it wont work - not even on boomers. Instead, embrace the new style of advertising that blends seamlessly into TikTok’s culture. e.g. instead of saying “I’m building Cue, the worlds first XYZ”, say “My Boss thinks I’m a 10X engineer. I’m actually not, I just record every meeting with Cue so I know what he wants from me” Here is an example: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7434489135655701792](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7434489135655701792)
  
- You’re still making ads on TikTok, just done beautifully with the right tone and fit with the existing community. Be bold with product placement but still be tasteful and natural.

### **Leverage Your Comments for Content**

- If a comment on your TikTok gets a ton of likes, immediately turn that comment into a TikTok. Whatever that comment says has already been validated by your audience to be an engaging thing. Think about how to turn it into a TikTok, and then actually turn it into a TikTok as soon as possible.
- **Use your comments section as an idea generator:** comment sections are basically a ranked list of content ideas based on real engagement (likes/replies). Use it to farm new ideas for content.

### **Stay In Tune with the Community & Platform**

- Respond to as many people as you can in comments. It keeps them coming back and tikTok see that those users return to watch the video a second time. That’s great for the algorithm. Respond to as many people as you can and treat everyone like a real human. The algo loves to see the additional engagement and will reward you + people love getting responses from creator accounts. These people then become advocates and bring new people.
  
- TikTok’s culture shifts daily. If you’re in tune with it, you’ll catch great opportunities. If you’re not, you risk missing the mark completely. Don’t stop keeping up to date with the content that your community is watching. Also more broadly, don’t miss out on trends. Any trend that’s going big, jump on it and make **multiple** pieces of content doing the trend, but fitted to your audience. Tiktok carries trend following videos further, because it instigates more users on their platform to become creators - ride these waves. here is an example: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7436342785663438112](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7436342785663438112)
  
- Use your competitors: If you have a competitor that everyone knows, use their name in your captions and content. it helps keep people for longer because they care about your competitor. Then over the course of watching your content, they’ll learn who you are and then start to care about you. Look at this namedrop of a competitor: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7431135711065132321](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7431135711065132321)

### **Encourage Discussion**

- Don’t just post pure ads. Your job on tiktok is to give new places (videos) where **the community can talk**. A simple question in your caption invites discussion in the comments. This is great for engagement and builds a stronger community.
  
- Write questions in your own comments section, or reply to the most liked comment with a question. It lowers the bar for people to comment (because they want to give their thoughts on your question) and invites more engagement. Again, the algo loves this.

### **Guide User Behaviour**

- TikTok users are **malleable**. If you tastefully ask for likes, you’ll get more. If you tastefully ask for favourites. This works especially if you make super super relateable content. Speak like your users and they will speak back, like this: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7432223901997976865](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7432223901997976865)

### **Put a Face to Your Content**

- People engage more when they see a face. Use yours (or someone else's) face **with emotion** in your content. Of course if you’re capturing POV footage, you can do without a face, but if you have a different type of video (like a gameplay video or meetings in office), add faces/reactions.

### **Timeless Content Strategies**

- The best Ideas don’t expire. If an ad worked in the 1930s, it still works today. Humans haven’t changed much. Find taglines that worked for companies in old ads and use them as hooks in your tiktoks. It works.
  
- Look at other platforms where your community are. Look at what they’re all talking about in the moment and then be creative and turn that into some kind of TikTok. The thing you’re making a TikTok about has already been validated to be what people want from those other platforms, so it has a high likelihood of working for you.
  
- **FOMO is a powerful driver.** Make people feel like they’re missing out. If you don’t have hype yet, fake it till you make it. Like this: [https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7428160129989725473](https://www.tiktok.com/@carter.chat/video/7428160129989725473) (we didn’t have millions of users)

### **Master the First 2 Seconds**

- The first **2 seconds of any tiktok** are by far the most important. Spend **90% of your planning time** on the first 2 seconds, and 10% on the rest.

- Users’ thumbs are locked and loaded to scroll and they make that decision within 2 seconds. give them a reason to stay.

### **Optimise for TikTok’s UI**

- Film with enough space for captions without blocking important visuals.
  
- Keep the focus near the centre of the screen**.** avoid placing crucial elements where TikTok’s UI will cover them.
  
- Make sure captions don’t spill behind UI elements like the profile icon or description. Captions should be non-obtrusive and unobstructed.

### **Perfect Your Lip-Syncing & Timing**

- If you're lip-syncing, get it right. take as many tries as you need.
  
- Go overly expressive instead of trying to look cool.
  
- Cut out every single **millisecond** of unnecessary timing. shave off the dull moments that cause viewer retention drops. The way you treat an onboarding flow should be the same way you treat every video. Be super sharp on the edit.

### **Prioritise Audio Quality**

- **Audio quality > Video quality** (unless bad audio is part of the joke).
  
- If your content requires clear audio (like a podcast clip), bad audio will kill it. Invest in a little bit of equipment at some point - DJI lapel mics, a decent shotgun mic for run n gun, decent camera for planned shoots (phone is fine for off the cuff content and most of your content, but if you’re planning a beautiful shot, make a bit of effort on the capture - it goes a long way).

### **Use Hashtags & Keywords**

- Find your community’s hashtags and use them. This helps TikTok categorise your content correctly.
  
- Farm for common search terms in the comments. write your product name in the comments so that it becomes a blue search term and people drive up your search volume inside tiktok. This helps your account rank well in the search results for similar search terms.

### **Tactical Comment Section Strategies**

- After posting, write the **funniest comment** on your own video.
  
- The goal: be the top-liked comment AND mention your product. This is actually the biggest traffic driver to your landing page.
  
- Like, comment, and favourite your own content to get the ball rolling.
  
- Be tactical: post comment chains, threads, and questions to boost engagement.
  
- Reply to everyone you can. this doubles your comment volume.

### **Leverage Every TikTok Feature**

- Use everything TikTok gives you: DMs, stories, live streams, etc. it all compounds over time.

### **Get Out of Algorithm Jail (aka "Bend the Knee to the CCP")**

- If your account gets shadowbanned, GO LIVE.
  
- Lives signal to TikTok that you’re engaged with the platform and can help recover reach.
  
- Getting "jailed" by the algorithm happens to **everyone.** this is the hack to fix it.

### **Use Strong Copywriting**

- Classic copywriting principles work best for any text on your account, including writing a script for a video or voiceover.
  
- Drop copywriting flames in your bio. it matters and leads to more link clicks.

### **Video & Audio Do’s and Don’ts**

- If you're **talking** in the video, avoid adding a background sound with lyrics—it can be **confusing**.
  
- Show that your **brand is run by real humans -** respond in a human way from your brand account. Duolingo’s comments and Ryanair’s comments are great to learn from.

### **Researching Content That Works**

Even though you should look at content that’s already working in your community, trust your own taste. **don’t just copy like for like**. Take the essence of the successful video and put your own spin on it. If you’ve done the work of understanding your audience on a granular level (inside jokes, other hobbies, their way of talking etc.) you’ll have more hits than misses when you come up with your own ideas.

### **TikTok Lives Are Gold**

Even if you don’t get many viewers, Lives boost your views for some reason. TikTok seem to like creators that go live. Your live audience can also turn fans into superfans, which is really useful when wanting to direct people to leave great reviews, fight haters etc.

### **Invest in Basic Equipment**

- Get a good mic, clean your camera, and buy a subscription to your editing platform of choice (we use premiere and capcut for little things)
  
- Look your best. TikTok is a visual platform.
  
- Film on an iPhone whenever possible instead of Android.
  
- Use proper lighting. Not too dark, not overexposed.
  
- IMPORTANT: When you have a finished video in your camera roll, put headphones in, put your volume at medium and listen to the video. Then at the same volume, open tiktok and scroll the fyp. If your video in camera roll is quieter at the same volume than your for you page, your video probably wont do well. Tiktok likes loud overexposed audio. you need to make your videos as loud as the for your page. Don’t post a video until you’ve done that test.

### **Keep It Simple**

- Overcomplicating shoots kills good content. Find the simplest way to convey your idea and just hit record.
  
- Take multiple takes—the first one is usually stiff.
  
- Warm up 5 minutes before filming so you’re loose and natural.

### **Become a First Mover**

- If something big is happening that your community cares about, Be the first to deliver news/opinions on it - establish yourself as an authority.

---

Once you’ve done all of the above for 1-3 months, you’ll be off to the races. Now it’s time to export all of your tiktok performance data, put it all into a spreadsheet and measure it. Sort your content by popularity and see if you can find patterns:

- We found early on that hitting a 10% like to view ratio gave a video a good chance of getting more views, so we made videos more relatable and easier to like early on. got a boost from that.
  
- We looked at the data again a few months later and found like ratios didn’t matter too much anymore, but instead getting a 1% favourite ratio mattered a lot, because favourite meant people really liked the content enough to essentially bookmark it to come back to. So we asked more people to favourite and got a boost from that.

By doing this tracking every few weeks, you’re actually keeping a stethoscope on the algorithm as it changes. Do this, and you’ll know what to focus on.

Repeat all of the above in a loop and you’ll get more than enough users to your product completely for free.
