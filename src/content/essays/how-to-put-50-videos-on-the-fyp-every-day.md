If you put 50 great videos on the FYP everyday, it’s unreasonable for you to not go viral.

That’s literally 90% of the game.

[Jess Hunt](https://www.tiktok.com/@jesshunt02) figured this out before she even started [Refy](https://www.tiktok.com/@refybeauty).

She was already a creator. She knew what content stopped people scrolling.

When she launched the brand, all she needed to do was to get 50x the volume of the content she was already posting.

But how do you get 50 videos a day as a startup without a massive ad budget?

Refy ran a UGC course teaching people how to become paid beauty creators.

They ran paid ads to get people to sign up, vetted candidates for who had the most promise and then ran the winners through personalised coaching on how to become a beauty creator.

Guess what their homework was…

Making content about Refy.

<img src="/images/essays/refy-content-loop.webp" alt="Diagram showing the loop between Refy’s content, its ambassador programme and more creators." width="1120" height="625" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

Jess showed these budding creators what worked on her account & got them all to replicate the same content with their own twist.

-   Don't over polish
-   Prop your camera up on your dressing table
-   Tell a relatable story
-   Do your eyebrows with a Refy product

et voila, 50 Refy beedeeoes on the FYP all fighting for attention. Every day.

Even if everybody only saw 3 of them, the volume and consistency makes you quickly go from ‘*what is this?*’ to ‘*how have i not heard about this?!*’

And then bank account goes up.
