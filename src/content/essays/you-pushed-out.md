Heard this saying from the legend [Uncle Shaan](https://www.shaanpuri.com/) - ‘You, pushed out.’

For anyone asking themselves:

-   What product should I sell?
-   What is my content strategy?
-   How do I cut through the noise?

Everybody’s approach to these questions is to ‘armchair theorise’ the right strategy (which doesn’t exist) - infinite Miro boards, Notion doc blah blah.

Instead, what if the answer to all of these was:

*“You, pushed out”*

The product you sell should make your friends say “That’s so you.”

The content you post should make your friends say “That’s so you.”

You get the jist…

Best example of “You, pushed out.” is [@strawberrymilkmob](https://tiktok.com/@strawberrymilkmob), content creator and founder of [https://strawberrymilkmob.com/](https://strawberrymilkmob.com/) - just go to this site & you’ll see exactly what ‘you, pushed out’ looks like.

Strawberry (or Georgia if we use her government name) and her sisters ‘Coconut’ and ‘Blueberry’ are some of the most authentic content creators on the internet right now. Every video they post tells you exactly who they are.

And their personality filters down from their content, to their shopify stores, to their product imagery, right down to their email copy.

The entire brand from top to bottom, is based on ‘You, pushed out.’

And they’re crushing it.

Don’t overthink your content. The only strategy you need is three words:

*You. Pushed out.*

/Danny
