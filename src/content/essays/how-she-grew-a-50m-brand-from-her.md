It’s 2 a.m.

I’m doom-scrolling my TikTok For You page, half-asleep, when I see it: a woman in a robe, fresh out of the shower, squeezing a tube of Refy Beauty’s glittery brow gel onto the back of her hand.

Why is this on my For You page? I have never been in the market for brow sculpting tools or any makeup, skincare stuff. I’m a monster trucks and MMA kinda dude.

But for some reason I watched the whole video of this woman doing her brows.

The sound and visuals are ASMR-level satisfying…*squish, squish, squish*…. I was completely hooked.

I scroll.

A few videos later, I see another woman doing her brows with the exact same Refy Beauty product and with the same exact ASMR hook.

Then again. And again.

By the fourth video of a woman doing her eyebrows in her bedroom with the SAME product, I rub the tiredness out of my eyes, prop up onto my elbows and lock in to figure out what the hell this brand is and why it’s taken over my FYP.

Little did I know, I was about to uncover one of the smartest, most creative organic TikTok growth strategies I’ve ever seen.

And here’s the best part: I realised this strategy doesn’t involve thousands of dollars, celebrity endorsements or flashy ad campaigns to work. It’s possible to replicate this strategy on day 1 of a business with zero funding.

Without further ado dear friends, grab a coffee (or a brow gel from Refy if you’re already convinced), and let’s dive into how Refy grew to millions in revenue through micro-influencers, ASMR, and the most underrated content format on TikTok—the *Get Ready With Me* (GRWM).

### 🎯 **The Refy Organic Growth Playbook:**

#### 1\. **The Micro-Influencer Blitzkrieg**

Most beauty brands throw their budget at one or two big-name influencers, cross their fingers, and pray for virality.

Not Jess (Founder of Refy) though. Jess is wayyy to creative and scrappy for that

Instead of paying £50K for a single celebrity post, she took a much smaller marketing budget and spread it across a tonne micro-influencers on an affiliate model (i.e. no money down, just commission on sales performance).

Overnight, Refy Beauty went from 0 to 50+ TikTok videos flooding the For You Page every single day.

Why does this work?

-   **Brute Force Volume**: TikTok’s algorithm thrives on repetition. If you see six Refy videos in a row (like I did), you’re not just intrigued—you’re *convinced*.
    
-   **Authenticity Over Glamour**: Micro-influencers feel like your mate, not a billboard. Their content is raw, relatable, and impossible to scroll past.
    
-   **Cost-Effective Virality**: Smaller creators are cheaper, hungrier, and often deliver higher engagement rates than their mega-famous counterparts. They just try harder than a big-name creator doing yet another sponsored post. Micro-influencers also are happy to work on a commission model instead of ‘money-for-content’, which can quickly become an unproductive money pit.
    

The result? Refy made £6M in sales in their first 10 months.

#### 2\. **The GRWM + ASMR Hook**

Here’s where Jess outsmarted all of her eyebrow competitors.

Most beauty brands post stiff, overproduced supermodel ads that scream “buy this.”

And nobody buy’s this…

Jess said ‘hellz to the no’ (I imagine) and instead told her creators: *“Girls, get out of the shower. Put on your robe. Prop your phone camera up on your dressing table. And film yourself doing your brows with Refy.”*

This is the birth of the *Get Ready With Me* (GRWM) marketing format, which is the sole driver of Refy’s early content growth. It just works because it’s so authentic.

But Jess didn’t stop there. She told creators to add a secret weapon for the hook of these TikToks: **ASMR**.

-   The sound of the brow gel squeezing out of the tube.
    
-   The brush strokes against skin.
    
-   The satisfying *click* of the product closing.
    

Remember, when someone scrolls onto a TikTok, their thumb is already spring-loaded to scroll to the next video.

You as a creator have about 2 seconds to keep that thumb from pushing your video off their screen.

For Jess’ product, she identified that ASMR visually satisfying shots of her glittery gel were the best performing hooks.

It’s weirdly addictive and it stops thumbs from scrolling.

Why does this work?

-   **Relatability**: People don’t want perfection. They want real life. A woman in a robe, doing her brows at 7 a.m., is infinitely more relatable than a airbrushed model.
    
-   **Sensory Appeal**: ASMR taps into a primal part of your brain. It’s not just content—it’s an *experience*.
    
-   **Shareability**: Satisfying sounds + relatable moments = pure bait for saves and shares.

### 🚀 **How to pull a daylight robbery on Refy’s growth strategy**

#### 1\. **Bet on Micro-Influencers**

Stop chasing big names. Find 50 creators with 10K-100K followers who genuinely love your product. Give them creative freedom. Pay them on sales performance so you don’t have to put money down. Watch the magic happen.

#### 2\. **Embrace the GRWM Format**

Scrap the polished ads. Show your product in action, in real life. Don’t forget about hooks. It’s the most important part. Stop that spring-loaded thumb from pushing your video away. Try ASMR, rage-baiting or anything else that proves to work.

#### 3\. **Flood the FYP**

One video won’t cut it. You need volume. Repetition breeds familiarity. Familiarity breeds trust. Trust breeds sales. Your task is to get 6 tiktoks on your target audience’s for you page within one day. This prints sales, so work backwards from this goal and figure out how to pull this off.

#### 4\. **Be Relentlessly Authentic**

People don’t buy products. They buy stories. Show them how your product fits into their life—messy, imperfect, and real, not stale and ‘ad-y’

### 5\. **Engage, Engage, Engage**

Reply to comments. Build a community. Make your audience feel seen. Replying to individuals is like compound interest. It seems like it doesn’t move the needle in the beginning, but over time you create superfans that become your loudest voices to new customers, simply by engaging with them on a human level.

### 🎬 **Final Thought: Jess Hunt is the Kylian Mbappé of Marketing**

Jess didn’t just create the Refy brand. She executed a masterful growth playbook to grow it. And the best part? It’s not exclusive to beauty. Whether you’re selling brownies, beef jerky, or brow gel, the principles are the same:

-   Bet on micro-influencers.
    
-   Embrace raw, relatable content.
    
-   Double down on the hook.
    
-   Flood the FYP.
    
-   Engage with the humans engaging with you.
    

So, what are you waiting for? Go film your eyebrows. Or your brownies. Or your beef jerky. Just start.
