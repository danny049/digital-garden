Look at the top 3 highest grossing movies of all time:

-   Avatar
-   Avengers: Endgame
-   Avatar 2

They're all based on a band of rebel good guys going to war with the bigger, stronger bad guys.

Same as the daily news - We're constantly shown a new war and a new narrative about who is good vs who is evil.

Picking teams in battle is one of our favourite things to do as humans…

And if we’re trying to grow a brand, Feeding into battles can really get people’s attention.

[Aimee Smale](https://www.tiktok.com/@aimeesmalex) did this - Founder of Odd Muse.

When Pretty Little Thing copied her product design, she formally declared them as opps and launched a full blown content war with them + got everybody on her side.
