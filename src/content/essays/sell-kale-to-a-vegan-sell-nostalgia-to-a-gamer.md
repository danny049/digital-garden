Imagine having a game that's such a great idea, it's hard *not* to go viral...

That's [Hypercharge:Unboxed](https://www.tiktok.com/@digitalcybercherries)

You play as a toy in a massive toy store overnight.

You wait till the night, break out of the packaging, pick a weapon and have an epic war with all the other toys.

We get so caught up in marketing strategy, that we forget to ask the question:

*'If i just pointed a camera at this thing, would people pay attention?'*

Some products are really hard to point a camera at, I get that.

But it's still a valuable question to ask

*What would this product need to look like to get attention all by itself?*

The more you can fit your packaging/aesthetics/vibe to what perform well on short-form, the easier your job on growth will be.

<u>Don't push water uphill if you can avoid it.</u>
