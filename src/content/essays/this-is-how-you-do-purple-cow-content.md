Seth Godin has this idea called ‘The Purple Cow’.

Essentially, You've seen a thousand cows that never caught your attention.

But one day on your drive, if you see a purple cow, you'll probably pull over to get a picture.

It's so visually striking and out of the ordinary, that you can't help but pay attention.

That's Liquid Death. They're a water brand that looks absolutely nothing like any other water brand on the planet.

Their water comes in a tallboy tinnie. That looks like a beer. With a heavy metal skull. Named after death.

Mike the Founder came up with the idea while watching a rock band dumping out beer cans and secretly pouring water into them, before going on stage

For some reason it’s an issue in the rock community to see a human being drink water.

Mike was pretty captured by what he saw. He posted a Facebook ad for ‘heavy metal water’ before the product even existed.

Woke up the next morning to hundreds of thousands of shares.

Turns out a lot of people wanted death water: Liquid Death is worth $1.4 billion today.

<u>Sell purple cows.</u>
