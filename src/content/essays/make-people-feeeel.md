-   Search for BetterHelp
-   Watch the videos and soak in the emotional feeling

Marketers often spend too much time on the ‘*meat*’ of the content, and not enough time on the '*sauce'* (aesthetics, colour grade, sound, tone of voice etc).

Betterhelp ads do both:

They have great copy, a great idea BUT they also have great LUTs, music, the introspective tone in voiceovers.

They make their ads actually feeeel like a hug, and that’s why their ads get so much engagement.

*Don't forget the sauce.*
