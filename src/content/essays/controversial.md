Controversy is one of the most powerful (but also dangerous) hooks that exist.

There’s a right way to do it, and definitely a wrong way

[Tony from LC sign](https://www.tiktok.com/@lcsign_lightbox) is the best example of the right way - LCSign is a Chinese LED shop sign manufacturer…

How the hell do you make that controversial?

Tony’s format of a funny controversial hook followed up by a direct ad read is genius.

Super repeatable, easy to scale, hyper engaging and the audience loves it.

I couldn’t find revenue growth data, but Tony has said in interviews that the TikToks have grown the business to the point where they are now supply constrained.

So yeah, apparently making fun of somebody’s long nipples can now add millions onto your top line 🤷‍♂️

Be careful though:

With great controversy, come’s great responsibility. When in doubt, ask:

*‘What would Tony do?’*
