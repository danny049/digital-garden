If a stranger watched *only one* video of yours, would they understand exactly what you’re about?

Like watching [@tooturnttony](https://www.tiktok.com/@tooturnttony) - One video tell you everything you need to know about this guy

and in turn, you'll remember him AFTER you've closed TikTok.

Is your content like this?

Or would most people forget what your video was even about 2 minutes later?

Any of your video’s that’s in the ‘forgettable’ bucket, re-do it and pack a heavier punch of ‘you’ or ‘your brand’ into that post.

The For You Page is the most competitive marketplace on planet earth.

It’s really hard to get your video onto someone else phone.

Even when you do, you have 1.6 seconds to impress or you're pushed away.

Your job as a content creator is to:

-   Stop that thumb from pushing you away
-   Be as memorable as you can while you have their attention.

<u>Pack a punch. No need to add filler episodes</u> 🙏

/Danny
