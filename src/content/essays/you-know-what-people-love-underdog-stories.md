People are so afraid to post their L’s online.

I get it, it’s uncomfortable.

But when you get slapped by life out of nowhere, sometimes turning that L into a whole public story, can deliver the coveted W.

[Lucie Macleod](https://www.tiktok.com/@luciemacleod), founder of Hair Syrup went on Dragons Den to raise £190k for her ecom brand and all 6 dragons said YES!!!

And then she woke up.

Because actually in reality, all 6 dragons said no.

So did Lucie just bury the L and move on?

Absolutely hell no - She turned the

*‘rejected by the establishment but i’m going to do it anyway’*

story arc into tonnes of hard hitting short-form content.

Hundreds of thousands of people on TikTok became passionate followers of Lucie’s David & Goliath story.

Fast forward to today, Lucie’s Hair Syrup is in Boots and heading for £15m in annual revenue.

The lesson is:

<u>If you're a small fish fighting bigger ones, </u> <u><em>say so</em></u><u>.</u>

People will pick a side. Make sure it's yours.
