James & Fuhad are so jokes.

We’ve all seen at least one <u><a href="https://www.tiktok.com/@shxtsngigs">Shxts n Gigs clip</a></u> on our FYP.

<img src="/images/essays/shxts-n-gigs.webp" alt="Three Shxts n Gigs clips, each with a host reacting on a phone to a funny post shown on screen." width="1120" height="630" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

They’ve grown to over 20M+ followers suuuper quickly which they just spoke about on this podcast [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mnjqsC1ahQc).

TLDR:

When no one knew who they were, they focused on one core thing:

Everybody that watches our whole TikTok need to be glad that they did. Not feel like it was a waste of time.

Thats really hard to do as a comedy podcast!!

You don’t know if your jokes are gonna land.

You don’t know if something funny to you, will actually be majorly offensive to the audience etc etc

So they decided to not start with telling their own jokes.

Instead, they found jokes and funny stories across the internet **that had already been validated by the audience to be hilarious**.

They read them to each other on camera, captured each other’s raw reaction and added on their own funny commentary.

Essentially, they 'shot the messenger(s)' 🎥

<img src="/images/essays/borrowed-trust.webp" alt="Diagram showing how a reaction video can borrow the audience’s trust from an existing viral clip." width="1120" height="625" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

If you’re trying to attract a specific audience but you haven’t *earned* their attention yet, go find things they already care about and attach it to your content.

I’ve personally gotten 50M+ views for my apps by doing exactly this.

Trust me, it works.

/Danny
